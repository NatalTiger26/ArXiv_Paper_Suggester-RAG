💡 How to Read This Subspace & Reference Points
Dominant Parent Dot (Red, 1, 0): The single foundational paper in the bibliography with the highest direct cosine similarity ($m$) to this paper.
Scattered Dots (Grey / Purple yⱼ): The other cited reference papers from the paper's bibliography projected onto this plane. Their positions show how each relates to the dominant parent vs the synergy direction.

📖 Discovery Platform User Guide & Architecture

1. Discovery & Multi-Signal Ranking

Search over 5,700 catalog papers or your research domain. Adjust weights to balance Embedding fit, Recency half-life, Global citations, and Author domain authority. Click any paper to copy its ID or inspect its subspace.

2. Citation-Grounded Q&A

Ask scientific questions and receive streaming answers with inline [1], [2] citations grounded in full-text chunks. Click any citation tag to jump directly to the supporting passage in the Source Passages tab.

3. 2D Novelty Cone & Foundational References

Enter any arXiv ID to project it into a 2D orthonormal reference subspace:

  • Red Dot (1, 0): The single closest foundational parent ($e_1$ baseline, cosine similarity $m$).
  • Blue Dot (x̂): Nonnegative linear synthesis ($\sum \lambda_j y_j$) inside the reference cone.
  • Scattered Dots (yⱼ): All other cited works. Indigo dots are active parents with $\lambda_j > 0$; grey dots are background citations.
  • Orange Dashed Line: Novelty residual gap ($\sqrt{1 - R^2_+}$) measuring departure from prior art to the unit circle.

4. Verified Authoritative Links

Every foundational reference provides direct external links to its official source: arXiv, DOI, or Semantic Scholar.
