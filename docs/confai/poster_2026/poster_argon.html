Live demo
Keyword search returns unranked lists. General chat models answer without pointing to the passages that support them.
Dense MRR falls 41.9% from 10 to 5,663 papers. Hybrid RRF adds +0.105 MRR (95% CI 0.015 to 0.197).
1  Find papers Which papers should I read first? Each paper gets a score from four signals: topic match, recency, citations, authors. You choose the mix.
2  Ask questions Can I trust answers from a big library? You pick the papers. The system retrieves passages and answers with numbered citations. We tested how well.
3  Judge novelty How original is a paper? We rebuild a paper from the papers it cites. What cannot be rebuilt is the part that is new.
Limitations
  • • Refusal precision is 40.6% (23.8% of answerable queries declined).
  • • Citation validity checks passage existence, not entailment.
  • • Gold set: 100 LLM questions on 10 papers, so added papers can score as misses.
Conclusions
  • • Dense MRR falls 41.9% and Hit@1 51.7% from 10 to 5,663 papers.
  • • Hybrid RRF adds +0.105 MRR over dense, CI 0.015 to 0.197.
  • • Cone novelty scores exceed an isotropic baseline (0.088 vs 0.017).
Future work
  • • Run hybrid retrieval across all sizes and test external novelty signals.
  • • In-person queries: gold generation, hybrid costs, reference coverage.
