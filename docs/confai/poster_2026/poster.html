1. Multi-Signal Literature Discovery
Ranking & 101k Graph Hubs

Keyword search in unranked catalogs floods researchers with obsolete or marginally relevant preprints. Our platform computes an inspectable multi-signal utility score balancing dense semantics, time decay, citation authority, and author continuity:

Score(q, p) = w_emb · S_emb(q, p) + w_rec · 2^(-Δt / t_half) + w_cit · (log(1+C) / log(1+C_max)) + w_auth · S_auth(p)
Where t_half = 4.0 yr exponential decay, C = global citation count, and S_auth measures domain author frequency.

2. Selective Ingestion & Hybrid RRF RAG
High-Precision Full-Text Grounding

Mass uncurated indexing degrades dense retrieval precision. Our workflow prompts the researcher to rank abstracts first, selectively downloading and ingesting only high-relevance full texts into Chroma:

Section-Aware Parsing
PDFs/HTML converted via PyMuPDF4LLM with OCR fallback. Split into 1,000 character target chunks (180 overlap) retaining markdown headings (## Methodology, ### Results).
Hybrid RRF & Section Priors
Combines dense embeddings (google/embeddinggemma-2) and BM25 lexical matches via Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Section-intent priors boost relevant sections based on query phrasing.
RRF_Score(d) = (w_dense / (60 + rank_dense(d))) + (w_lex / (60 + rank_lex(d))) + Prior(section, q)
Passage-Level Verifiable Synthesis (Gemini 2.0 / 3.8 Flash)
The generator prompt strictly forces answers to anchor statements to exact passage numbers: [1], [2]. ConfAI evaluation measures 98.9% citation validity (89 of 90 cited links verifiable against retrieved text chunks).
3. 2D Reference Cone Geometry
Subspace Innovation Decomposition

Does a new paper truly break ground, synthesize disparate lineages, or simply make an incremental step? We project normalized paper vector x into the convex cone of its cited references Y = [y_1, ..., y_k] via Non-Negative Least Squares:

λ* = argmin_{λ ≥ 0} || x - Y λ ||²,     x̂ = Y λ*,     R²_+ = || x̂ ||² ∈ [0, 1]
Orthonormal Canvas Basis:
• X-axis (e_1): Aligned to closest parent y_{m*}, plotted at (1, 0).
• Y-axis (e_2): Orthogonal synergy vector u / ||u||.
• Synergy Blend gain: Δ = R²_+ - max(m, 0)².
• Novelty Residual: Res = √max(0, 1 - R²_+).

Honest Operational Limits (AI in Practice): (1) Refusal calibration remains an open boundary: conservative safety gating leads to 23.8% false refusals on marginal contexts.
